# Temple Run 2 — data-driven C rewrite

Gameplay core rebuilt from the shipped APK (`com.imangi.templerun2` 1.137.0,
Unity 2022.3 / IL2CPP) and the decompiled C# / serialized assets. This replaces
the earlier hand-modelled runner with the game's own track-piece corpus,
pacing tables and trigger volumes.

```
./build.sh templerun2 --metal     # ./puffer (Metal trainer)
./build.sh templerun2 --cpu       # ./templerun2 (interactive raylib)
./puffer train                    # config/templerun2.ini
```

## Recovered behavior encoded here

- **Track:** `TR2_PIECE_TABLE`/`TR2_VARIATION_TABLE` from `TrackPieceInfo`;
  traversal follows the shipped `GeneratedPath`, falling back to `SourcePath`.
  Base junctions use both authored branches. Variations use their extracted
  frequencies and avoid immediate repeats when another playable variation
  exists. Full-width pits are deferred until their rim/FallSaver is reachable
  at the current speed, including decision-timing margin. Sub-mode pieces
  come from the MineCart/WaterSlide/Forest pools.
- **WaterSlide:** the shipped entrance and exit bracket a random chain of 2–5
  flume pieces, so runs range from short slides to long rivers. The runner is
  shown lying on the flume; lateral swipes move between lanes and the
  duck/swipe-down gesture lowers the pose under the authored water-block
  volumes. Standing through one hits it. Jumping cancels the duck, matching
  `Jump() -> StopUnderWaterSliding`.
- **Zipline:** the rope must be caught with a jump. A grounded runner that
  reaches the zipline piece misses the rope and falls; an airborne runner
  attaches at rope height, hangs with the shipped `ZiplineIdle01` clip, and
  drops under gravity when the piece ends. Lane, jump and slide are disabled
  while attached.
- **Pacing:** exact Machu (RegionID 100) 8-milestone table: obstacle spacing
  10–20 → 0–2.5, turns 25–50 → 5–25, coins 80–40, double obstacles 0–0.20.
- **Runner:** speed 10→21, force buckets `30/10/1.5/0.5 × 0.1`, ground gravity
  −25 with airborne ×2, jump velocity clamp 8–10, slam 20, slide
  `min(speed·0.07, 0.75)`, stumble ×0.85, lane servo 10%/frame, bounded to
  ±0.85 m from the path centre; a second unprotected stumble within 10 seconds
  is lethal.
- **Collision:** real collider volumes from `colliders.json`; runner sphere
  r=0.44; shield = 10 s/one hit, boost = 250 m + 4 s invulnerability.
  Free-fall bypasses both; invulnerability protects against ledge triggers.
  Gaps remove ground support instead of acting as solid walls. The extracted
  FallSaver window can rescue a short jump with a stumble; walking/sliding
  into the opening still falls.
  Water has its own observation kind rather than masquerading as a block.
  Its trigger exempts rising jump entry on non-turn pieces, riding and
  invincibility; shields do not protect against water. Continuous overlap
  does not replay the entry event when the runner starts descending.
- **Items:** coin values 1/2/3 at 0/2500/5000, mega 50, magnet 10 s/√1200,
  gem +1, score formulas from shipped data.

## Interface

The four gestures are left, right, jump, and slide. The policy also has an
idle choice. `decision_frames = 2`: each 30 Hz decision sends one swipe event,
then an idle physics frame, rather than holding or repeating that gesture.
Physics/collisions and rendered playback remain at 60 Hz. Lane swipes retain
a 120 ms debounce. Training samples the policy; Metal evaluation can select
its highest-probability legal action with `--base.eval_deterministic=1`.
`swipe_cost` is optional and defaults to zero; no action is masked.
Common base-track models are warmed before gameplay. Slow uploads/inference
can drop intermediate pictures to catch up; physics and policy events are
never skipped. Do not run GPU training alongside a latency-sensitive demo.
The portrait window scales down to fit shorter displays without cropping the
gameplay or HUD.

Observation: 238 floats — 27 base state (lane, jump/slide, speed, score, items,
mode, current piece flags/timers), 3 turn, 6 pieces × 4, 10 obstacles × 15
(nine hazard kinds, collider width, length and vertical bounds), 6 coins × 4, and
2 powerups × 5.

Reward: `reward_clear` for each obstacle passed without a hit and `reward_coin`
per coin value; swipe cost and collision/death penalties. Distance, powerups and
turns do not add RL reward, though they retain their gameplay and score effects.
The default clear/coin/death/swipe rewards are 0.125/0.01875/−1/0;
their scale preserves the intended death-to-clear ratio under trainer clipping.
`env/coins` reports coins collected per completed episode.

## Verification

- `tests/test_templerun2_env.c` covers controls, decision batching, reachable
  course generation, collision/reward boundaries, and pickups.
- `tests/test_templerun2_gap.c` covers large-gap jumps, failed approaches and
  FallSaver rescues using the extracted volumes.
- `./build.sh templerun2 --metal` builds `./puffer`.
- Playback smoke: 360 physics frames in 6.015 wall seconds (59.85 effective
  physics FPS), with warmed assets and no concurrent training.
- Renderer loads exported track models and an animated runner with shipped
  clips. Visual parity is incomplete: scenery and props can overlap or be
  misaligned, and some materials and camera effects differ from the game.

The observation width is now 238, including a separate water-hazard channel.
Earlier 168/228-float checkpoints cannot load and must be retrained. Policies
trained at the old one-decision-per-physics-frame cadence also need retraining.

## Scope

The core endless run and obstacle/coin/powerup systems are approximated.
Unity UI, ads, IAP, audio, missions and upgrades are not reproduced. The five
Machu zone pools are present; zone progression/fork selection is simplified
to category-based piece selection. Sub-mode sequences and collision timing
are not exact. In particular, the modelled jump cannot clear the
`jump_or_slide_a` fire volume, despite its name; the simulated collider
currently requires a slide.

The previous analytic implementation is kept at `ocean/templerun2_legacy/` for
comparison.
